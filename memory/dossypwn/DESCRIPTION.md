Like everything else, and more than most, DOS was especially susceptible to memory corruption vulnerabilities!
Lacking any mitigations whatsoever, it could truly have been a hacker's wonderland.
However, the exploitation of memory corruption vulnerabilities was not so well understood, and outside of mostly-just-annoying viruses, mayhem did not really manifest.

From our lofty position in the future, we can look back and change that!
This challenge is a peek into the vulnerability of DOS applications.
Go and learn what once was, and what hackers did not even understand until it was long gone.
